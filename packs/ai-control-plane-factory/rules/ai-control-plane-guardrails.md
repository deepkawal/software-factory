# AI Control Plane Guardrails

- Route AI traffic through a governed endpoint when workloads touch sensitive,
  regulated, production, or proprietary data.
- Do not allow teams to bypass runtime policy with direct model-provider calls.
- Keep AI system registry, AI Bill of Materials, policy versions, and release
  evidence aligned with production configuration.
- Require company-owned audit logs when policy, regulation, incident response,
  or customer commitments require internal ownership.
- Prefer golden-path controls that make compliant AI usage easier than ad hoc
  exceptions.
