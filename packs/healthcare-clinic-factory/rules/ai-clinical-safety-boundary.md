# AI Clinical Safety Boundary

AI may assist clinic staff only inside an approved clinical workflow boundary.

## Default Controls

- Clinician-facing summaries are advisory unless formally approved otherwise.
- Source data must remain accessible for verification.
- Output must not create orders, diagnoses, billing events, or patient-facing
  instructions without approved human review.
- Prompt, completion, trace, and evaluation retention must be reviewed before PHI/ePHI use.
- Failure mode must be safe: no summary is better than a misleading summary.
